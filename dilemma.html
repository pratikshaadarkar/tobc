"Dilemma" unravels the complexities of relationships and the haunting aftermath of a traumatic incident. Vicky, entangled in a long-term relationship with Shweta, is shaken by a surreal nightmare that echoes the disturbing reality Shweta faces. The narrative unfolds as Vicky, shocked by the dream, confronts Shweta about her tardiness, only to unearth the brutal truth behind her delayed return.

Shweta unveils a horrifying tale of rape and abandonment, transforming Vicky's initial frustration into disbelief and fear. The couple engages in a heated argument, exposing deep-seated issues in their relationship. Shweta's desperate plea for understanding clashes with Vicky's struggle to reconcile with the harsh reality.

The story takes an unexpected turn when it loops back to the beginning, leaving Vicky in a state of confusion. Is it a recurring nightmare, or is it a glimpse into an alternate reality? The lines between dream and reality blur as Vicky grapples with the nightmarish possibility of Shweta's ordeal.

In the end, the couple faces the chilling prospect of reliving their tumultuous confrontation. "Silent Dilemma" explores the fragile nature of relationships, the impact of trauma, and the eerie boundaries between dreams and reality. The silence that follows the shattered plate symbolizes the broken fragments of their relationship, leaving the audience with a haunting and thought-provoking conclusion.
