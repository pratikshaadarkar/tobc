TOBC started with a song in 2021.

We met wherever we could. No business plan. No office.

Just a music video called Tum Mere Paas and one belief: that good work finds its audience.

We manage talents. We build brands. We show up on set, on time, and on brief. Every single time.

That's how a song built a production house.
