We're The Out Of Box Creators Entertainment,
And Our Friends Call Us TOBC

TOBC started the way most real things do. Not with a business plan, but with a dream.

Shivam Singh wanted to make a film. So he started where every good story starts, with a song. Tum Mere Paas was TOBC's first music video, and it opened a door that hasn't closed since.

From that first shoot, TOBC grew into something nobody handed us. A Mumbai-based production house trusted by brands like Philips, PolicyBazaar, Rapido, ITC Hotels, and Skyscanner, and agencies like Tring, The Minimalist, IndieVisual, Colatch and Dentsu. We've stood on set with Alia Bhatt, Tamannah Bhatia, Saina Nehwal, Ali Fazal and Sunil Grover.

None of it was handed to us. Every client came back because we showed up. On time, on budget, and at a quality they didn't expect for the margins we work with. Repeat clients aren't a metric at TOBC. They're proof.

Today, TOBC is a full service Mumbai based production house with 5+ years of experience, 200+ projects, 100+ clients and 100+ campaigns delivered. From music videos and TVCs to ad films, we handle everything from pre-production to post-production and marketing. We work with brands, ad agencies, artists and creators, with everything managed in-house by one team to maintain a consistent standard across every project.

We make work worth remembering. And we make it on time.
